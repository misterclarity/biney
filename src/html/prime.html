Beats before you work: pick one and 5 to 20 minutes of priming. When the bell rings, start, with the beat quieter, pink noise, or silence.

Why before, not during

The clearest finding in the meta-analysis is about timing: listening before a task, or before and during it, worked better than listening only while you work. So prime first, get a cue when the block ends, then start.
