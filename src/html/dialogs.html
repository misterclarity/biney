Quick headphone check

A binaural beat only exists if each ear receives its own tone. On speakers, a single earbud, or a mono Bluetooth device the two tones mix before they reach you and nothing on this page does anything. This takes five seconds.

    Settle

    The session has ended and the sound is off. Keep your eyes open, and feel your feet on the floor and the seat under you.

    Then look around you: name five things you can see, four you can touch, three you can hear, two you can smell and one you can taste.

    If this keeps happening, or a practice often leaves you upset, talk to your doctor or a mental-health professional before you carry on with it. If you feel unsafe right now, call your local emergency number.

    Match your tinnitus pitch

    The notch has to sit on your tinnitus to do anything. Move the slider until the tone sounds closest to the pitch you hear, then save it. Keep the volume low — this is a pure tone.

    4000Hz

    Matches are commonly out by a whole octave, and which way depends on where you started from — so check the octave above and below before you settle.

    Above 8 kHz the notched-training trials found no significant effect. You can save this pitch, but expect the notch to do less than the trials reported.
    See a clinician first. Tinnitus that is new, one-sided, pulsing in time with your heartbeat, or paired with hearing loss or dizziness needs medical assessment. Sound therapy is an adjunct to care, not a substitute for it, and not a cure.

    Before the light pulse

    • It flickers 40 times a second. Flashing light can set off a seizure in people with photosensitive epilepsy, sometimes their first, and it's commonest in children and young people: keep it away from children.
    • End it at once, with a tap anywhere, if you feel unwell, dizzy or sick, get a headache, see odd colours or patterns, or notice a twitch. Flicker can bring on a migraine in some people: with migraines, ask your doctor first.
    • Sit down in a well-lit room, with the phone at arm's length, and look at the glow or near it. Never while driving, cycling or walking.
    • It ends by itself after ten minutes, when the sound stops, if the screen falls behind, or when Entrain goes out of sight.
