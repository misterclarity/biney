Quick headphone check

A binaural beat only exists if each ear receives its own tone. On speakers, a single earbud, or a mono Bluetooth device the two tones mix before they reach you and nothing on this page does anything. This takes five seconds.

Match your tinnitus pitch

The notch has to sit on your tinnitus to do anything. Move the slider until the tone sounds closest to the pitch you hear, then save it. Keep the volume low — this is a pure tone.

4000Hz

Matches are commonly out by a whole octave, and which way depends on where you started from — so check the octave above and below before you settle.

Above 8 kHz the notched-training trials found no significant effect. You can save this pitch, but expect the notch to do less than the trials reported.
See a clinician first. Tinnitus that is new, one-sided, pulsing in time with your heartbeat, or paired with hearing loss or dizziness needs medical assessment. Sound therapy is an adjunct to care, not a substitute for it, and not a cure.
