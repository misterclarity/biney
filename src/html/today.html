    Before you start

    • {i:sounds}Use headphones for the beats. On speakers the two tones mix in the air and the binaural beat disappears.
    • {i:volume}Keep it quiet. Beats come through best at a low, comfortable level.
    • {i:warn}Read the safety notes once. Who should ask a doctor before using the beats or the breath holds, and how to wear headphones with a pacemaker.

    Why a restaurant?

    The word comes from the French restaurer, to restore: the first restaurants, in 18th-century Paris, served restorative broths. Entrain serves breath instead. (And entrain is French for gusto.)
