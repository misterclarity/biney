    Before you start

    • {i:sounds}Use headphones for the beats. On speakers the two tones mix in the air and the binaural beat disappears.
    • {i:volume}Keep it quiet. Beats come through best at a low, comfortable level.
    • {i:warn}Not for everyone. Avoid the beats if you have epilepsy or a seizure disorder, wear a pacemaker, or are driving or operating machinery. This is not a medical device.
